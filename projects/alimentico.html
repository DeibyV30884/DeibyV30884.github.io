AlimenTICO · Food Donation Platform

Platform that connects restaurants with surplus food to people who need it, to reduce waste and create social impact.

My role: Team project. I analyzed the requirements, proposed what would be built and split the work into parts. I also developed the largest share of the system.

Notice: Restaurants, beneficiaries, donations and emails in the video are demo data; they do not correspond to real businesses or people. Note: the interface shown in the videos is in Spanish.

My role and scope

Roles
Restaurant, beneficiary and administrator
Technologies
PHP with MVC pattern, MySQL, Docker and PHPMailer
Scope
Donations, reservations with a pickup code, notifications and restaurant opening hours

What it is made of

Restaurant

  • Dashboard with donation summary
  • Publish and edit donations
  • Donation details
  • Profile with hours and location

Beneficiary

  • Available donations with search and filters
  • Details and reservation
  • My reservations with pickup code
  • Profile

Administrator

  • Dashboard with statistics
  • Beneficiary management
  • Restaurant management
  • Profile

Access

  • Sign in
  • Restaurant and beneficiary registration
  • Password recovery by email

PHP with the MVC pattern (controllers, models and views), a MySQL database and email sending with PHPMailer. It runs in Docker containers.

What the video shows

  • Restaurant: publishes a food donation, reviews its donations and manages its profile with hours and location.
  • Beneficiary: searches available donations, views the details, reserves one and checks the pickup code.
  • Administrator: overall summary, management of beneficiaries and partner restaurants.
