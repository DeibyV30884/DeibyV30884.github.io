Appointments and Medical Records System

Role-based web application to schedule appointments, keep patient records and register treatments and prescriptions.

My role: Team project. I analyzed the requirements, proposed what would be built and split the work into parts. I also developed the largest share of the system.

Notice: Patients, appointments, records and users in the video are demo data and fictitious; there is no real medical or personal information. Note: the interface shown in the videos is in Spanish.

MVC application on Spring Boot with Thymeleaf views, route- and role-based security with Spring Security, and a MySQL database. The interface can be switched between Spanish and English.
